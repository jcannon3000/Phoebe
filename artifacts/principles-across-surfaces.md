# Phoebe — Principles across surfaces
### Read this before any individual surface brief.

This document sits **above** the per-surface briefs (progress UI, Customizer, Fellows/circle, illness mode). Those briefs say *what to build on each screen*; this one states the *laws every screen obeys*. Where a surface brief and a principle here ever conflict, **this wins**. Several of these were learned the hard way and **reverse earlier decisions** — that's noted where it happens. The order is roughly by depth.

---

## 1. Pull, not push (the deepest law)
**A surface is something a person reaches *toward*, never something that reports *on* them.**

Why: removing the *number* from a tracker does not make it safe. The wound is not the score — it's the *gaze*. A surface that lets others (or even yourself) see whether you kept the practice creates a place to be found wanting, which lands hardest on exactly the lonely, regard-hungry person Phoebe exists to hold. Pull/push catches what "presence not score" missed.

Build / don't:
- A shared circle carries **needs and offers only** — *pray for me tonight · I'm holding you · I prayed with you*. It shows **nothing** about whether anyone kept the rule.
- **Reverses earlier work:** retire shared compliance-visibility entirely — including the Turn/Learn/Pray "lights." Visible devotion among peers is a net wherever people want each other's regard, which is *most* among close friends, not least. The lights do not ship in any peer context.
- Reminders: a reminder that **calls you to** the practice at its hour is a bell (good — it lowers the threshold to begin). A reminder that fires **because you fell short** is a deficit pushed at you (kill it, every surface — no shortfall, streak-risk, or "you haven't yet" notification).
- Even self-facing surfaces lead with the **invitation forward** (the next anchor), not the backward ledger of what you did.

Bites hardest: the circle/Fellows surface, and every reminder in the app.

## 2. The rule is a gift, not an achievement
**Guard the *meaning* of the practice as fiercely as the mechanics.**

Why: the office's whole mercy is that it asks nothing but to be received, points at God and not the self, and **cannot be done well or badly**. The moment it's framed as self-improvement it's been colonized by the optimization machine already crushing the user — one more thing to be good at.

Build / don't:
- **Never** frame the rule (in copy, onboarding, reminders, or notifications) as self-care, focus, stress relief, productivity, or thriving.
- **Reverses earlier wording:** "BUILDING YOUR DAILY HABIT OF PRAYER · 7/11" — drop "habit" and the n/11 completion meter; that's an optimization frame narrating the *authoring of a rule*. Use receiving-language ("shaping your rhythm" / "your rule of life").
- Default to **receiving** a coherent rule (named starter rules), not building one from a blank screen. Receiving is the literal opposite of optimizing.

Bites hardest: the Customizer (all copy), onboarding, reminder text.

## 3. Two instruments, not one stretched across both
**The community/planting mode and the illness/threshold mode are separate builds that must not inherit each other's chrome.**

Why: you build *down* from the hard case, never *up* from the light one. The death-register and non-instrumental-under-pressure can only be forged where death is; the reached-toward circle and rule-as-gift are the planting end. Fusing them corrupts both.

Build / don't:
- **Community/college mode:** reached-toward circle (no compliance lights), rule-as-gift, planting. **No death-register.**
- **Illness/threshold mode:** a held center who *receives*, a steward, holds death and mourning. Intercession flows *toward* the center — this is the one place "seen presence" is allowed, because it is care for a receiver, **not** peer surveillance of devotion. **Never** grows a peer-compliance surface.
- Same DNA (the offices, praying-with, the deacon-carrier identity); two builds; neither borrows the other's mechanics.

Bites hardest: any temptation to reuse college mechanics at the bedside, or bedside gravity in the dorm.

## 4. Position, not score (now subordinate to #1)
**Every self-facing indicator answers "where am I in the day?", never "how much have I done?"**

Why: still true and still load-bearing for the *self* surfaces — but note it was **insufficient on its own**. Where peers can see each other, #1 supersedes it: position-not-score fixes the self ledger; only pull-not-push fixes the gaze.

Build / don't: no "N of M" meters, no fill bars, no checkbox/✓ completion grammar, no quota on contemplation. Day-arc and "next anchor," not a depleting tally.

Bites hardest: the home / Daily progress surfaces.

## 5. The body forms it; the thread only carries it
**Relationships form in person; the app sustains them, never manufactures them.**

Why: any thread can deliver the *appearance* of accompaniment without the body. The retreat (or the gathering, or the priest at the bedside) is the body; Phoebe is the thread between. Reverse the order and you simulate bonds instead of deepening them.

Build / don't: circles are **chosen in the room, never assigned** by the app. The *for* grows out of the *with*. Phoebe's job begins *after* the human connection exists, carrying it across the gap the body can't reach.

Bites hardest: circle formation (the September retreat), any "suggested connections" instinct.

## 6. Spine and vine
**Prayer is the spine of the rule; supports ride alongside.**

Why: the offices and contemplation are the trellis; an imported habit (a language streak, a podcast log) is at most a vine on it. Rendering them identically flattens the rule.

Build / don't: prayer practices (offices, contemplation, reflections, lectio, examen) are the spine; everything else — including built-in extras — rides in a quieter "also in your day" band. Make the distinction **structural, never moral** (the vine is welcome; it's simply not the trellis). Deliberate exception to decide on purpose: gratitude / journaling, which some rightly feel *are* part of their rule.

Bites hardest: the Customizer authoring moment and the home's "Also today" band.

## 7. Scaffolding built to recede
**Structure helps most early and should quiet as a practice settles.**

Why: a tool that stays equally loud trains dependence — the engagement incentive Phoebe refuses everywhere else.

Build / don't: progress chrome de-emphasizes as an anchor is kept over time; "Customize" splits into full *authoring* (first time) and quiet *tending* (re-entry), and the entry point itself grows less prominent once a rhythm has held. The training wheel's success state is coming **off**.

Bites hardest: the Customizer re-entry, the home's progress prominence.

---

## The one test (applies to every surface)
Does this screen let a person **reach toward** God and their people — or does it **report on** them? Reaching is a gift. Reporting is a net. When in doubt, build the version that can only be reached toward.

## What this governs
The per-surface briefs implement these laws on specific screens:
- **Progress UI brief** → principles 1, 4, 7
- **Customizer brief** → principles 2, 6, 7 (and 1 on its reminders)
- **Fellows / circle** → principles 1, 3, 5 (the lights are retired by #1)
- **Illness / threshold mode brief** → principle 3 (the separate, harder instrument)

If a surface brief predates this document and contradicts it, this document is the correction.
